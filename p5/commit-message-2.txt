P5 (t_bc967b65): the oracle cannot separate the two attention paths - every divergence is at a margin the
oracle itself moves on, so the 2.21x stays refused

Twelve engine arms (shipped prompt attention vs STRATA_PROMPT_ATTN_OLD=1's batched decode attention, x int8/fp16
KV, x 4K/32K/128K, 256 greedy tokens), the independent oracle (llama.cpp-SYCL qwen4exp, same IQ3_S file, f16 KV,
-ub 2048) on the same prompt-id files at the two lengths it can reach, and the oracle's own -ub 512 instantiation
of those same prompts as the measurement of the reference's own resolution.

Measured:

* the A/B reproduces P2 byte for byte (4K ad985c23, 32K 124a3cd3, 128K c60e72d3, old-attn 32K c52712f4) and the
  speed: prefill 213.6 -> 281.3 (1.32x) / 342.8 -> 765.2 (2.23x) / 411.5 -> 883.0 (2.15x) tok/s, qsa attn 75.1%
  -> 31.6% of the 32K chunk GPU timeline and 65.6% -> 22.6% at 128K.
* every divergence of ours from the oracle sits at an oracle top1-top2 margin of 0.0120-0.1731 nats, and the
  oracle's own -ub 512 arm emits OUR token at 4K index 78 (264, 9640, 24277, 4621 at a 0.1731-nat margin) and at
  32K index 75 (3992 at 0.0120) - the reference changes its own mind at exactly the positions we differ on. The
  one divergence the oracle is stable on (32K/101, 0.0436 nats, odds 1.045:1) is absent from the batched-fp16 arm
  and present in the other three.
* precision vs algorithm: the 32K first divergence moves 101 -> 75 when the KV goes int8 -> fp16 (the int8
  cluster at 101 is the KV's), while at 4K the batched arm is BYTE-IDENTICAL with int8 and fp16 KV (md5 9428de79
  both) and the shipped arm with fp16 KV is byte-identical to the oracle - the one reproducible kernel difference.
  The kernels' own parity vs FP64 (ctx 3832/32768, both KV modes): 1.3-2.2e-06 and 1.2e-06 of scale apart from
  each other, so both are FP32-accurate and neither is convicted.
* the 128K oracle does not exist on this box: two attempts at -ub 2048 die at 49,152 of 129,024 prompt tokens with
  UR_RESULT_ERROR_DEVICE_LOST (once in FLASH_ATTN_EXT, once in mul_mat_id), after the prompt rate falls 484 ->
  174 tok/s with depth.

Verdict: a TIE, with the raw metric favouring the shipped path (4K fp16 148/148 exact against the batched arm's
78/79 at a 0.1731-nat flip; 32K int8 first divergence 101 against 75) and the band-aware metric favouring the
batched one (the only divergence no oracle instantiation reproduces is absent from batched-fp16). With no length
where the fast path is certified, the ship condition is not met and the default configuration is unchanged.
