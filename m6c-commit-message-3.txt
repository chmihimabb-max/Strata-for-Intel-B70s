m6c: IQ3_S served at the full 262,144-token window on both B70s with KV streaming, and the depth curve

The card's deliverable, measured 2026-10-02 on the project box with the config of record
(strata-sycl-iq3s.json: "gpu": [0,1] -> --layer-split auto, ZE_AFFINITY_MASK unset, --kv int8,
--expert-cache auto, --mmap-experts, --spec 4, --no-capture, --stats) plus --kv-resident 32768:

Depth curve, --prefill auto, 256 usage-counted generated tokens per row (both cards, IQ3_S GSQ-RCO):
  prompt 32,256   prefill 344.8 tok/s   decode 21.71 tok/s   TTFT  93.70 s
  prompt 64,512   prefill 339.6 tok/s   decode 18.67 tok/s   TTFT 190.05 s
  prompt 129,024  prefill 342.6 tok/s   decode 19.55 tok/s   TTFT 376.74 s
  prompt 259,943  prefill 316.9 tok/s   decode 18.45 tok/s   TTFT 820.52 s   <- the full window
--prefill 512 (the second arm, and what M6/I1/I2 used) is 1.47x/1.56x slower at 32K/64K with decode
unchanged: the chunk size is a measured prefill term, and it is why the card names --prefill auto.

KV streaming engaged and measured, not assumed: kv_resident=32768 cells per QSA layer (8,192 pages of 4
cells; a no-op at 32K by construction), 1.29 GiB pinned per stage at 262,144 cells, and 90.99% of 790,765
block reads served from VRAM with 287.1 MiB read from RAM over the 256K request.  The RAM cost is
12,672 B per context token (12.4 KiB) against the docs' 13.7 KB, which counts 13 QSA layers where the
model has 12.

On this box streaming does NOT buy expert residency -- 24,576 slots against 24,302 (+1.1%) at 256K, both
100%/-99% resident -- and it costs 18.9% of the prompt read (316.9 against 390.7 tok/s) while decode moves
3% the other way.  Upstream's 1,589 -> 3,872 experts was a 12 GB card; here the K/V (3.32 GB) is 5% of the
63.8 GiB of VRAM and the expert profile fits either way.  Stated as a finding, as the card asked.

SSD tier: the 83.62 GB file sat in the page cache (Cached 90 GB of 123 GB), the warm 256K runs still read
10.32 GiB from the device once the engine's own 48 GiB RSS squeezed the cache, and the page-cache-dropped
arm read 54.45 GiB with 73,864 major faults while prefilling at exactly the same 316.9 tok/s.

Correctness at depth: the needle at 50% depth is answered exactly (ZK-4471-QX) at the 4K control and is
NOT retrieved at 256K (the model concludes no such code is in the document) -- the negative result the
card asked not to paper over.  A 5%-depth arm of the same 256K prompt locates it (see the write-up), and
the oracle differential was attempted but this fork crashes at 262,144 with a quantised KV cache.

Evidence: m6c/ in the repo (harness, tables, all runs) and ~/strata-xpu/WRITEUP.md Part II.
