m6c: the curve rows are a code-review chat turn, not a document continuation

A plain continuation of the corpus is not a usable depth-curve prompt: at 64K the model wrote the
end-of-turn token after 62 generated tokens (DONE 62 64512 286513.5 3125.9 stop ...), which fails the
card's ">=256 usage-counted generated tokens at every length".  The curve rows are now the shape
upstream's table used - a real code-review task over long source files: a system/user/assistant chat whose
user turn is the 32K/64K/128K/260K document and whose task is to list the correctness and performance
risks in it.

Also adds the --prefill auto arm at 64K: upstream measured their table with --prefill auto (the engine's
own help: the largest chunk up to 8192 whose buffers the expert cache can lend), so whether our
--prefill 512 is part of the prefill gap becomes a measurement instead of a guess.

The four runs measured with the continuation shape are kept under m6c/runs-plain-continuation/.
