D2 (t_0416a0c0): the arm rig, the tensor census, and task 0's first arms

The rig is D1's arm runner retargeted at d2/runs (one engine, the config of record
verbatim, a FIFO so the ask is fed after "everything loaded"), plus the two chains:
chain0 = task 0's --spec-min-p 0.5 vs 0.7 re-verification at 4K/32K/128K, chain1 =
the dispatch A/B arms (batched vs per-token decode GEMV, the three hyper-connection
read variants, the fused vs per-token mixer read, and the launch-site census).

d2/D2-TYPES.txt is the tensor -> type census the dispatch map is read against: the
GGUF shard's own types per tensor class, from tools/gguf_reader.py (the same types the
engine's NativeDense reads), plus the pack's per-layer expert gu/down types.  300
native dense projections and 48 layers of routed experts, and the type of every one of
them is now written down rather than assumed.

The binary under every arm is build-sycl/strata (md5 e79b2ad632d37d32786228b660d35b29),
i.e. the config of record's exe at HEAD; the arms' own logs carry the md5 they ran.
